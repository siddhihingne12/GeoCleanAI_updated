# Shivajinagar - Emerson Tower and Patil Estate visual review

Reviewed 13 September 2026. **Not approved for annotation: no confirmed roadside dump or overflowing bin in 47 distinct reviewed images.**

The initial screen requested three evenly distributed recent frames from each of 11 sequences and produced 33 perspective images captured from 28 October 2024 to 5 March 2026. A pile beside an auto-parts shop looked ambiguous in one frame, so the sequence was checked with nine distributed frames and nine consecutive frames centred on that image. Accounting for overlap between manifests, the complete review covers 47 distinct images. These are screening observations, not training labels.

The adjacent sequence resolves the ambiguous pile. Sacks, cardboard and tarps are stacked above and behind the auto-parts shop as stored workshop material. A separate pile farther along the same frontage consists of broken tiles and masonry. Both are useful difficult negatives, but neither matches the project’s household-waste roadside-dump class.

Several adjacent frames show loose wrappers and small debris along the shopfront. The material is sparse or dispersed and remains below the dump threshold. No overflowing bin is visible. Traffic, buses, auto-rickshaws, motorcycles, backlight and motion blur limit part of the sample.

The metadata rectangle contains 1,444 recent eligible images, so this review does not prove that every recent frame or the wider Shivajinagar area lacks waste. Four sequence-centroid clusters estimate route separation; they are not confirmed waste sites or validated train/test groups.
