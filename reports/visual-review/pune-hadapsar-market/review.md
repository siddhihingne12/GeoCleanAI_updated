# Hadapsar Mantri Market - Gadital visual review

Reviewed 13 September 2026. **Not approved for annotation: no confirmed roadside dump or overflowing bin in 18 initial samples.**

Three evenly distributed recent frames were downloaded and individually inspected from each of six sequences. All samples are perspective images, captured in March 2025 or March 2026. These observations are screening notes, not training labels.

Some images show shopfronts and market stalls. Upright sacks beside a coconut vendor do not establish discarded waste. Broken road surface and stone debris remain potential negative examples. A small patch of leaves and scraps beside parked scooters falls short of a substantial dump. A small ordinary green container shows no clear overflow.

Flyover barriers, traffic, low camera aim and motion blur limit much of the sample. Map positions inside a market-area rectangle do not guarantee views of market ground. No clear target justified denser target-follow-up sampling under the current screening process. This does not prove that all 717 recent frames or the wider Hadapsar area lack waste.

Keep the evidence and move to queue entry 6, Dhole Patil Road. Training remains on hold until a corridor supports suitable examples of both classes. Sequence-centroid clusters are not confirmed waste sites or validated train/test groups.
